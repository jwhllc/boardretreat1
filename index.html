The reality: Running consecutive deficits is not sustainable. We have ~9 months of liquid runway (~15 including endowment) before we need to make hard decisions about program scope and staffing. This retreat is about creating the urgency and strategy to close that gap.

Financial Trend: FY24–FY26

FY24
Strong: $6.1M raised
Healthy

A banner year. We had major gifts, strong board giving, and successful grant awards. Assets grew to comfortable levels, and we were positioned for expansion.

FY25
Deficit: Major expansion
~18 mo

Strategic investments. We made a deliberate choice to invest in growth. We expanded our Airline campus operations and launched comprehensive in-school programming across multiple high schools. Both initiatives require upfront staffing and program costs. We funded this through reserves, knowing it would create a short-term deficit but position us for long-term impact and revenue growth. Individual gifts and grants also dipped somewhat in the wake of our MacKenzie Scott funding.

FY26
Deficit: Continued growth
~15 mo

Growth continues, runway tightens. FY26 continued the investments we started in FY25. Staff salaries, program costs, and operational expenses grew with scale. We expected to receive the Connor Group gift, which did not materialize, and other anticipated major gifts came in below expectations. We ran another deficit and drew down assets. Current runway is tight: 15–18 months depending on revenue scenarios.

FY27 Fundraising Plan

We're building predictable, repeatable fundraising initiatives. This is how we'll engage you, steward donors, and generate the revenue we need.

Fundraising Calendar

ONGOING • OCT–APR
The BRYC House Series. Board-hosted gatherings, up to two per month, running alongside everything below. Plus ongoing 1-on-1 cultivation with prospects year-round.

You sign up for a month. We'll pick the exact date together — whatever works for you.

You invite 8–15 friends or colleagues who could give. We provide the email and text language.

You provide the space and cover food & drinks. Your home, your office, or a private room — your vibe, your call. Need logistical support? We'll work it out case by case.

We handle everything else. Materials, facilitation, the presentation, and the ask. You get people in the room; BRYC seals the deal.
